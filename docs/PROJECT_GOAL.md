# ACGPS Project Goal

## Mission

Create an independent **AI Coding Good Practice System** that automates the disciplined development of complex software—from idea and approved product intent through specification, architecture, planning, implementation, verification, independent review, release-candidate preparation, and operation—while requesting human input only when human values, domain authority, risk acceptance, irreversible consequences, or external authorization are genuinely required.

## Primary users

- A human Product & Risk Owner managing several complex AI-assisted projects.
- High-capability Planner and Reviewer models.
- Coding models executing bounded work packages.
- Verification agents and deterministic tools.

## Target project types

ACGPS must support research-heavy and decision-intensive projects such as:

- FTIC: frontier-technology intelligence;
- FIC: forecasting and calibration;
- GSIS: strategic intelligence and decision support;
- GAPS and GIQTS: portfolio and quantitative investment systems;
- HSIPS: commercially viable, humanly meaningful story-development systems.

## Durable outcomes

1. A repeatable, auditable development lifecycle.
2. Explicit human/model decision boundaries.
3. Risk-adaptive automation rather than blanket supervision or blanket autonomy.
4. Trigger-based use of specialized skills.
5. Independent planning, coding, review, and verification roles.
6. Evidence-backed state transitions and completion claims.
7. Low-token cross-session continuity through concise authoritative files.
8. Reusable project profiles without embedding domain logic in the ACGPS core.
9. Frozen review and release baselines with traceable hashes.
10. Progressive evolution from CLI orchestration to optional multi-agent automation.

## Success definition

ACGPS is successful when a complex project task can enter as a structured intake, proceed through the correct design, implementation, verification, and review gates, stop only for policy-defined human decisions, and produce a reproducible delivery or a precise evidence-backed reason why delivery is blocked.
